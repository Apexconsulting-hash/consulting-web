Apex Consulting operates as a professional corporate advisory service. All custom solutions, automation audits, and integration programs are executed based on individualized client Statement of Work (SOW) agreements. We do not charge credit cards upfront for vague services; all clients receive a formal, signed SOW detailing precise deliverables and hourly rates before any payment is requested. Because our engineering allocations are tailored specifically to the operational infrastructure of each enterprise, we adhere to the following fulfillment and refund policy to guarantee professional transparency.

1. Service Fulfillment & Onboarding Timeline

  • Fulfillment Initiation: Digital service onboarding, technical discovery, and initial project diagnostic workflows commence within three (3) to five (5) business days of verified payment processing as authorized under your active contract.
  • Project Delivery: All standard project phases, hourly architecture development, and advisory audits are executed according to the engineering timelines explicitly defined in your customized Client Statement of Work (SOW).

2. Refund & Mid-Project Cancellation Terms

  • Completed Consulting Hours: Fees for consulting hours already completed and delivered are strictly non-refundable once engineering analysis or documentation has commenced.
  • Mid-Way Project Cancellation: If an active project is canceled mid-way by the client, the client will only be billed for the authenticated consulting hours logged up to the official cancellation date. Any unspent retainer fields or unlogged hours will not be charged.
  • Milestone Payments: Hourly segments tied to fixed project milestones (as outlined in your SOW) are non-refundable once the respective milestone phase has been initiated and logged.

3. Subscription & Monthly Retainer Cancellations

  • Notice Period: Clients operating on an ongoing monthly advisory or systems management retainer may cancel their agreement at any time by providing at least thirty (30) days written notice prior to the next scheduled billing cycle.
  • How to Cancel: All cancellation requests must be submitted formally via email to support@apexconsulting-hash.com.
  • Prorated Fees: If notice is given fewer than 30 days before the next billing cycle, the next scheduled hourly step will process normally, and advisory services will remain fully active for that final 30-day period. No prorated refunds are issued for partial months outside of documented hourly bounds.
