Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Due to the highly customized, resource-intensive nature of enterprise operations architecture and advisory work, payments for completed discovery phases and delivered milestone phases are non-refundable. Clients may terminate ongoing monthly advisory agreements by providing 30 days written notice directly to support@apexconsulting-hash.com.

1. Service Fulfillment & Onboarding Timeline

Fulfillment Initiation: Digital service onboarding, technical discovery, and initial project diagnostic workflows commence within three (3) to five (5) business days of verified initial payment processing.

Project Delivery: All standard project phases, architecture development, and advisory audits are executed according to the timelines explicitly defined in your customized Client Statement of Work (SOW).

2. Refund Terms

Custom Advisory & Diagnostic Audits: Payments for completed technical infrastructure reviews, discovery audits, and custom operational frameworks are strictly non-refundable once delivery or data analysis has commenced.
